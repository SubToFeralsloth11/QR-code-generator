About

QR Code Generator is a free, no-account-needed tool that lets you create QR codes for any URL — websites, YouTube videos, Vimeo links, and more. Everything runs directly in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

Why we built this

Most QR code generators require signing up, charge for downloads, or watermark your QR codes. We wanted something simple: paste a link, get a QR code, download it. No strings attached.

What you can do

  • Generate QR codes for any website or video URL
  • Customize colors to match your brand or style
  • Adjust the size and error correction level
  • Download as PNG, SVG, or PDF
  • Share a link that opens the generator with your URL pre-filled

How it works

The QR code is generated entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your URL never leaves your device — we don't have a backend server processing data. The libraries we use (qrcode and jsPDF) run client-side, meaning your data stays private.

Technology

This site is built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No frameworks, no build tools, no database. Just static files served fast. The QR code rendering is powered by the open-source qrcode library, and PDF export uses jsPDF.
